# Data contract

The pipeline writes a few JSON files into `app/public/data/`. The app loads them at start, except the deferred lever files, which it loads once a lever needs them. The reference for each file's shape is its TypeScript type. The core files' types are in `app/src/data/types.ts`, and each lever file's type is in the `data.ts` of the lever module that reads it (`app/src/levers/<id>/data.ts`). A change to a file's shape changes its type and this page in the same commit.

Each file is written by one section of the pipeline, `pipeline/planetshift_pipeline/sections/<section>.py`, registered in `SECTIONS` in `build.py`.

| File | Type | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `meta.json` | `Meta` | `pipeline/planetshift_pipeline/sections/meta.py`: the base year, the build date, the departures from FaIR of `planetshift.config.json` it was built with and every source |
| `countries.json` | `Country[]` | FAOSTAT population, area codes, World Bank income groups |
| `items.json` | `Item[]` | FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheets, `pipeline/groups.csv` |
| `supply.json` | `Supply` | FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheets |
| `intensities.json` | `IntensitiesFile`, read as `Intensities` | HESTIA aggregations, FAOSTAT emissions, published LCAs; seafood from FAO FishStat with Parker et al. (2018) and MacLeod et al. (2020) |
| `feed.json` | `Feed` | feed allocation tables, FAO FishStat fed aquaculture for `seafood` |
| `land.json` | `Land` | FAOSTAT harvested area and land use, each country's land area (`landArea`, optional: without it the map's land use layer is empty), natural regrowth rates |
| `breakdown.json` | `Breakdown` (`levers/practices/data.ts`) | emissions by source (enteric, manure, rice, fertiliser…) from HESTIA terms and FAOSTAT |
| `practices.json` | `Practices` (`levers/practices/data.ts`) | published reductions of farming practices; for Clean farm energy, the kWh drawn per kg of fuel CO₂ switched (FAOSTAT GN), each country's grid intensity over the world's (Ember, else FAOSTAT GN) and FAOSTAT's world grid for a build without `sectors.json` |
| `waste.json` | `Waste` (`levers/waste/data.ts`) | food waste after shops |
| `deforestation.json` | `Deforestation` (`levers/deforestation/data.ts`) | deforestation CO₂ attributed to commodities, and the forest area cleared (`areaByCountry`, optional: without it the map's Deforestation layer shows no clearing) |
| `removals.json` | `Removals` (`levers/removals/data.ts`) | carbon stored in farmland by practice |
| `deadfall.json` | `Deadfall` (`levers/deadfall/data.ts`) | CO₂ that large pelagic fish left uncaught would sink to the deep ocean, kg per tonne less of each seafood item by country, from FAO FishStat's catch and Mariani et al. (2020)'s sinking fractions, with the deep-water share and the build-up's time constant it assumes |
| `sectors.json` | `Sectors` (`levers/energy/data.ts`) | fossil CO₂ by sector under each SSP, grid intensity, EV electricity, data centres, aviation non-CO₂, forcing response |
| `population.json` | `PopulationData` (`levers/population/data.ts`) | world population from 1850 (Our World in Data's long-run series, then UN WPP 2024), WPP 2024's medium, low and high projections, and each country's low and high over its medium |
| `people.json` | `PeopleData` (`levers/population/data.ts`) | read by the *People on Earth* slider: each SSP's human emissions above their 1750 level (RCMIP), kg a year, with the parts from fossil fuels and industry and from land use as the causes of warming count them (`byCause`), the forcing of their air pollution (FaIR's coefficients), W/m², and each SSP's own population (AR6 Scenario Database) |
| `industry.json` | `Industry` (`levers/industry/data.ts`) | direct fossil CO₂ of buildings and industry under each SSP, heat-pump electricity, steel, cement and chemicals (ammonia left out) shares, cuts and electricity |
| `coemissions.json` | `CoEmissions` (`levers/energy/data.ts`) | the CO₂ of producing the fuel, methane, N₂O and air pollution (SO₂, black and organic carbon, ammonia, NOx, CO, VOCs) per kg of each sector's fossil CO₂ under each SSP (CEDS, RCMIP), aviation's own as `aviationOwn`, their forcing per kg and the methane a kg of NOx or VOCs leaves in the air (FaIR), NOx's for each value of the `noxMethane` departure |
| `climate.json` | `Climate` | FaIR, PRIMAP-hist, HadCRUT5, Berkeley Earth: the warming by cause and, in `attributionByGas`, each greenhouse gas's warming by the causes that emit it; the permafrost feedback's constants (AR6) and the 2020 shipping sulphur rule's forcing by SSP (`shippingSulphur2020`, declared in `levers/shippingSulphur/data.ts`) |
| `landuse.json` | `LandUse` | each country's net CO₂ from land-use change, the mean of the Global Carbon Budget's three bookkeeping models (BLUE, OSCAR, LUCE) over 2015–2024, and the world's |
| `enso.json` | `Enso` | NOAA's Oceanic Niño Index: one value a year (the mean of the seasons centred from the previous October to September) and the year's phase, El Niño, La Niña or neutral, at ±0.5 °C |
| `deeptime.json` | `DeepTimeFile` (`app/src/deeptime/series.ts`) | the deep-time prototype only, fetched when its view opens: PhanDA's 85 stage means with 5–95%, CENOGRID as global temperature from IPCC AR6 Cross-Chapter Box 2.1, extended before 33.5 Ma with the same formula on CENOGRID's PANGAEA bins (66 Ma to 24 ka, 10,000-year means), LGMR's 200-year means with 5–95% (mean ± 1.645 SD) and PAGES 2k from IPCC AR6 Figure 2.11a (1–1849 CE). Years are CE, temperatures °C relative to 1850–1900, and `offsets` holds what was added to each series to put it on 1850–1900 |
| `impacts.json` | `Impacts` | the card *What the warming means*: four groups of impacts, each with the global warming levels its source assessed them at (°C above 1850–1900) and whether both futures must lie within them (`jointly`, for the sources that stop at 2 °C). Each row has a unit and a reference, and a central value (`mid`), a range (`low`, `high`) or both at each level: IPCC AR6 WGI Figure SPM.6's frequency of extremes (CEDA, the median and 5–95% of the models), AR6 WGII SPM B.4.1, SR1.5 SPM B.3.1, B.4.1, B.4.2 and B.4.4, and Dosio et al. (2018), the last three from `pipeline/impacts.csv` |
| `mammals.json` | `Mammals` | farmed mammals' live weight in tonnes by country (FAOSTAT's livestock stocks in its emissions domain, in `year`, times IPCC 2006's typical animal mass by region from `pipeline/mammals_live_weights.csv`), split by the food group each herd follows (`ruminantMeat`, `dairy`, `pigMeat`) and `other` for the animals not raised for food; `wild`, the world's wild land and marine mammals in tonnes of wet weight (Greenspoon et al. 2023), and that study's address in `source` |
| `species.json` | `Species` | species committed to extinction per hectare of cropland and pasture by country, `[average, marginal]` (Scherer et al. 2023's country factors for land occupation, light use on cropland and light or minimal use on pasture by its ruminants' livestock units per ha, times each group's described species from the IUCN Red List 2025-1); `described`, the species of the five groups; `fish`, the biomass of the fish fisheries target without fishing and at the global peak catch, tonnes (Bianchi et al. 2021). Optional: without it the page has no species or fish figures and no species layer |
| `world.topo.json` | TopoJSON | Natural Earth admin-0, 1:50m, with `iso3` on each feature |

The ten lever files (`breakdown`, `practices`, `waste`, `deforestation`, `removals`, `deadfall`, `sectors`, `coemissions`, `industry`, `population`, each declared by its module's `files`) are loaded with the rest. One that is missing (HTTP 404) is replaced by the empty value its module gives, so its levers have no effect and the interface shows them as not available. Without `population.json`, for example, the population option has no effect and the temperature chart draws no population. `enso.json`, `landuse.json`, `impacts.json` and `mammals.json` are optional core files (`OPTIONAL_FILES` in `app/src/data/load.ts`). Without the first, the temperature chart has no El Niño or La Niña marks; without the second, the map's land-use CO₂ layer has no data; without the third, Explore has no card on what the warming means; without the fourth, the summary has no farmed mammals figure and the map's farmed mammals layer has no data. `climate.json`'s `attributionByGas` is optional too: a build without it hides the gas switch of the Causes of warming chart, which then shows every cause. Any other file missing stops the app. A file its module marks `deferred` (`breakdown.json`, 2.2 MB, read by the farm practices only) is not loaded at start. Its empty value stands in for it until one of its module's levers is above 0, or until the panel works out what each lever is worth, in idle time after the page has settled. The interface does not wait for it to show those levers. As the app cannot know at start whether a deferred file is in the build, a practice lever stays available without `breakdown.json` and has no effect. `removals.json` rates are in tonnes of CO₂ per hectare a year, `deadfall.json` in kg of CO₂ per tonne of the item, `deforestation.json` in tonnes of CO₂ a year and its `areaByCountry` in hectares a year, `landuse.json` in tonnes of CO₂ a year (negative for a net sink, its `world` holding what the source assigns to no country), `population.json` in people (its ratios have no unit), `coemissions.json` in kg per kg of CO₂, W/m² per kg a year and kg of methane per kg of NOx or VOCs. Without `coemissions.json` the energy and industry levers cut CO₂ alone.

`intensities.json` stores each distinct source once, sorted, in `sources`, and each cell's `source` is its index there (`IntensitiesFile`). The loader (`decodeIntensities` in `app/src/data/load.ts`) puts the URL back in each cell, so the model reads `Intensities`, whose `source` is the URL. Its `oilcake[iso3][oilseedCode][scope]` gives the kg of each gas a year that the cake of each crushed oilseed carries in the base year. This is the part of the crush's emissions that an oil taken from its seed leaves out (see `docs/methodology/en/emissions.md`). The loader keeps it in `Intensities`, and the model adds it to the country's farm emissions and changes it with the demand for the cake, as `oilcakeHa`. A build without it counts no cake emissions. Its `nonFood[iso3]` gives the kg of each gas a year from the country's animals raised but not farmed for food (draught cattle and buffalo, horses, asses and mules, cattle kept other than for food), the same in both scopes. The model adds it to the country's farm emissions, and no lever or shift changes it. A build without it has no such baseline.

Units: masses in tonnes for products and kilograms for gases, temperatures in °C relative to 1850–1900, years as integers. Country keys are ISO 3166-1 alpha-3, item keys are FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheet item codes as strings.

The fixtures in `app/public/fixtures/` (made by `npm run fixtures` in `app/`) follow the same types with a handful of countries and items, so the model and the interface are tested without the real data. `?data=fixtures` loads them in the app, and the end-to-end tests use it.
