# Data contract

The pipeline writes a few JSON files into `app/public/data/`. The app loads them at start, except the deferred lever files, which it loads once a lever needs them. The reference for each file's shape is its TypeScript type. The core files' types are in `app/src/data/types.ts`, and each lever file's type is in the `data.ts` of the lever module that reads it (`app/src/levers/<id>/data.ts`). A change to a file's shape changes its type and this page in the same commit.

Each file is written by one section of the pipeline, `pipeline/planetshift_pipeline/sections/<section>.py`, registered in `SECTIONS` in `build.py`.

| File | Type | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `meta.json` | `Meta` | `pipeline/planetshift_pipeline/sections/meta.py`: the base year, the build date, the departures from FaIR of `planetshift.config.json` it was built with and every source |
| `countries.json` | `Country[]` | FAOSTAT population, area codes, World Bank income groups |
| `items.json` | `Item[]` | FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheets, `pipeline/groups.csv` |
| `supply.json` | `Supply` | FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheets |
| `intensities.json` | `IntensitiesFile`, read as `Intensities` | HESTIA aggregations, FAOSTAT emissions, published LCAs; seafood from FAO FishStat with Parker et al. (2018) and MacLeod et al. (2020) |
| `feed.json` | `Feed` | feed allocation tables, FAO FishStat fed aquaculture for `seafood` |
| `land.json` | `Land` | FAOSTAT harvested area and land use, each country's land area (`landArea`, optional: without it the map's land use layer is empty), natural regrowth rates, each country's albedo forcing per hectare of farmland where forest would grow back (`albedoPerHa`, W/m² of global mean, optional: without it regrowth changes no albedo), and the oil each oilseed gives with the oil's share of the crush's value (`crush`, from `pipeline/oilcakes.csv`; without it, the food footprint counts all of an oilseed's clearing on the seed) |
| `breakdown.json` | `Breakdown` (`levers/practices/data.ts`) | emissions by source (enteric, manure, rice, fertiliser…) from HESTIA terms and FAOSTAT |
| `practices.json` | `Practices` (`levers/practices/data.ts`) | published reductions of farming practices; for Clean farm energy, the kWh drawn per kg of fuel CO₂ switched (FAOSTAT GN), each country's grid intensity over the world's (Ember, else FAOSTAT GN) and FAOSTAT's world grid for a build without `sectors.json` |
| `waste.json` | `Waste` (`levers/waste/data.ts`) | food waste after shops |
| `solidwaste.json` | `SolidWaste` | municipal solid waste by country, World Bank What a Waste 3.0: `byCountry[iso3]` with `tonnes` generated in `year` (2022, the World Bank's estimate), `kgPerPerson`, `income` (`high`, `upperMiddle`, `lowerMiddle`, `low`), `types` (shares of `foodGreen`, `paper`, `plastic`, `glass`, `metal`, `textiles`, `other`) and `treatment` (shares of `landfill`, `openDump`, `incineration`, `recycling`, `composting`, `other`, `uncollected`), each adding up to 1, and `filled`, which of the two were taken from the country's income group. Optional: without it the map's municipal waste layer and the Waste chart's two municipal views are empty |
| `oceanplastic.json` | `OceanPlastic` | plastic reaching the ocean through rivers, tonnes a year by country (`byCountry[iso3]`), Meijer et al. (2021) through Our World in Data, for `year`. Optional: without it the map's ocean plastic layer is empty |
| `deforestation.json` | `Deforestation` (`levers/deforestation/data.ts`) | deforestation CO₂ attributed to commodities, and the forest area cleared (`areaByCountry`, optional: without it the map's Deforestation layer shows no clearing); the CO₂ and N₂O of drained organic soils (`drainedByCountry`, `drainedN2oByCountry`) and the CH₄ they would emit once rewetted (`rewettedCh4ByCountry`), tonnes a year by country and item, all three optional |
| `removals.json` | `Removals` (`levers/removals/data.ts`) | carbon stored in farmland by practice |
| `deadfall.json` | `Deadfall` (`levers/deadfall/data.ts`) | CO₂ that large pelagic fish left uncaught would sink to the deep ocean, kg per tonne less of each seafood item by country, from FAO FishStat's catch and Mariani et al. (2020)'s sinking fractions, with the deep-water share and the build-up's time constant it assumes |
| `sectors.json` | `Sectors` (`levers/energy/data.ts`) | fossil CO₂ by sector under each SSP, grid intensity, EV electricity, data centres, aviation non-CO₂, forcing response |
| `population.json` | `PopulationData` (`levers/population/data.ts`) | world population from 1850 (Our World in Data's long-run series, then UN WPP 2024), WPP 2024's medium, low and high projections, and each country's low and high over its medium |
| `people.json` | `PeopleData` (`levers/population/data.ts`) | read by the *People on Earth* slider: each SSP's human emissions above their 1750 level (the calibration's history to 2022, then RCMIP), kg a year, with the parts from fossil fuels and industry, land use and waste as the causes of warming count them (`byCause`), the forcing of their air pollution (FaIR's coefficients), W/m², and each SSP's own population (AR6 Scenario Database) |
| `industry.json` | `Industry` (`levers/industry/data.ts`) | direct fossil CO₂ of buildings and industry under each SSP, heat-pump electricity, steel, cement and chemicals (ammonia left out) shares, cuts and electricity |
| `wastemethane.json` | `WasteMethane` (`levers/wasteMethane/data.ts`) | CEDS's landfill and wastewater methane by country in `year` (2022), kg, with each country's `managed` share of buried waste that goes to landfills (What a Waste 3.0), and every waste sector's for the world (`total`); the waste cause's methane under each SSP (`ch4`, kg a year, as climate.json's *Waste*); the ranges of `capture`, `diversion` and `wastewaterCut` at full adoption, and `decayYears`, the time constant of buried waste's decay |
| `coemissions.json` | `CoEmissions` (`levers/energy/data.ts`) | the CO₂ of producing the fuel, methane, N₂O and air pollution (SO₂, black and organic carbon, ammonia, NOx, CO, VOCs) per kg of each sector's fossil CO₂ under each SSP (CEDS, RCMIP), aviation's own as `aviationOwn`, their forcing per kg and the methane a kg of NOx or VOCs leaves in the air (FaIR), NOx's for each value of the `noxMethane` departure, and in `coMethaneSplit` the VOCs', CO's and the chemistry variant of NOx's methane for the `split` value of the `coMethane` departure |
| `hfc.json` | `HfcData` (`levers/hfc/data.ts`) | each SSP's emissions of the eleven HFCs FaIR runs from 2020 (`emissions[ssp][specie]`, kg a year, as `fair.json` has them), each one's yearly `decay` and `relative` forcing per kg over HFC-134a (the `carrier`) from FaIR's calibration (`cycles`), its AR6 GWP100 (`gwp100`), and the share of every HFC the lever cuts at 100% (`cut`) |
| `fossilmethane.json` | `FossilMethane` (`levers/fossilMethane/data.ts`) | the methane leaked producing oil and gas (`oilGas`) and coal (`coal`), CEDS's fugitive sectors: the world's under each SSP (`emissions`, CEDS in `cedsYear` moved as RCMIP's energy methane), its part of `coemissions.json`'s methane per kg of each sector's CO₂ (`perKgCo2`), each country's in `cedsYear` (`byCountry[iso3]`), and the share of each the levers remove at 100% (`avoidable`) |
| `climate.json` | `Climate` | FaIR, PRIMAP-hist, HadCRUT5, Berkeley Earth: the warming by cause (land use's CO₂ as `landUse` and its albedo change as `landAlbedo`, RCMIP's state-based albedo forcing, and `waste`) and, in `attributionByGas`, each greenhouse gas's warming by the causes that emit it, methane's forest, grassland and peat fires as `burning`, the HFCs' (`hfc`) all under `other`; `agtp`, each gas's AGTP curve by method, HFC-134a's (`hfc`) included; the permafrost feedback's constants (AR6) the 2020 shipping sulphur rule's forcing by SSP (`shippingSulphur2020`, declared in `levers/shippingSulphur/data.ts`), and the sea-level rate per °C and balance temperature fitted to the median and each likely end of AR6 Table 9.9, and the observed global mean sea level from 1900 (`seaLevel`, optional; `observed`, AR6's tide gauges then NASA's satellites, m relative to 1995–2014), and each SSP's CO₂ in the air from FaIR with its response to a kg emitted, for ocean pH (`co2` in ppm and `co2PpmPerKg` in ppm per kg, optional), and the observed ocean pH from the Copernicus Marine Service (`oceanPhObserved`, optional) |
| `fair.json` | `FairFile` | the FaIR run in the app (`app/src/model/fairClimate.ts`): FaIR's species and climate configs for the 50 members of `pipeline/fair_members.csv` after FaIR 2.2.5 has read the fair-calibrate v1.4.1 calibration (`speciesConfigs`, `climateConfigs`, each array given once when every member holds the same values), the solar and volcanic forcing at year boundaries from 1750, and each SSP's emissions of every FaIR species in FaIR's units and land-use albedo forcing in W/m², one value per calendar year from 1750 to 2100 (`ssp`) |
| `landuse.json` | `LandUse` | each country's net CO₂ from land-use change, the mean of the Global Carbon Budget's three bookkeeping models (BLUE, OSCAR, LUCE) over 2015–2024, and the world's |
| `enso.json` | `Enso` | NOAA's Oceanic Niño Index: one value a year (the mean of the seasons centred from the previous October to September) and the year's phase, El Niño, La Niña or neutral, at ±0.5 °C |
| `deeptime.json` | `DeepTimeFile` (`app/src/deeptime/series.ts`) | the deep-time prototype only, fetched when its view opens: PhanDA's 85 stage means with 5–95%, CENOGRID as global temperature from IPCC AR6 Cross-Chapter Box 2.1, extended before 33.5 Ma with the same formula on CENOGRID's PANGAEA bins (66 Ma to 24 ka, 10,000-year means), LGMR's 200-year means with 5–95% (mean ± 1.645 SD) and PAGES 2k from IPCC AR6 Figure 2.11a (1–1849 CE). Years are CE, temperatures °C relative to 1850–1900, and `offsets` holds what was added to each series to put it on 1850–1900 |
| `impacts.json` | `Impacts` | the card *What the warming means*: four groups of impacts, each with the global warming levels its source assessed them at (°C above 1850–1900) and whether both futures must lie within them (`jointly`, for the sources that stop at 2 °C). Each row has a unit and a reference, and a central value (`mid`), a range (`low`, `high`) or both at each level: IPCC AR6 WGI Figure SPM.6's frequency of extremes (CEDA, the median and 5–95% of the models), AR6 WGII SPM B.4.1, SR1.5 SPM B.3.1, B.4.1, B.4.2 and B.4.4, and Dosio et al. (2018), the last three from `pipeline/impacts.csv` |
| `mammals.json` | `Mammals` | farmed mammals' live weight in tonnes by country (FAOSTAT's livestock stocks in its emissions domain, in `year`, times IPCC 2006's typical animal mass by region from `pipeline/mammals_live_weights.csv`), split by the food group each herd follows (`ruminantMeat`, `dairy`, `pigMeat`) and `other` for the animals not raised for food; `wild`, the world's wild land and marine mammals in tonnes of wet weight (Greenspoon et al. 2023), and that study's address in `source` |
| `species.json` | `Species` | species committed to extinction per hectare of cropland and pasture by country, `[average, marginal]` (Scherer et al. 2023's country factors for land occupation, light use on cropland and light or minimal use on pasture by its ruminants' livestock units per ha, times each group's described species from the IUCN Red List 2025-1); `described`, the species of the five groups; `fish`, the biomass of the fish fisheries target without fishing and at the global peak catch, tonnes (Bianchi et al. 2021). Optional: without it the page has no species or fish figures and no species layer |
| `water.json` | `Water` | freshwater withdrawn per tonne of each item by country, `byCountry[iso3][itemCode]` = `[litres per tonne, tier, index into sources]`, from HESTIA's crops (tiers 1 and 2: on-farm withdrawals plus those of making the non-agricultural inputs), Poore & Nemecek (2018) through Our World in Data (tier 4, converted from per kg of retail product to per tonne of the FBS item; animal products less the water of their concentrate feed), proxies and processed items (tier 5) and group medians (tier 6), as the emission tiers; `scarcity[iso3][itemCode]`, HESTIA's AWARE 2.0 scarcity-weighted use in L eq per tonne, only for the crops with the country's own aggregation and never added to litres; `oilcake[iso3][oilseedCode]`, the litres a year a crushed oilseed's cake carries (the oilseed's share of the crush's value that the oil does not take); `irrigatedHa[iso3]`, FAOSTAT's land area equipped for irrigation (RL item 6690), ha. Seafood items are 0: fish farms' freshwater is not counted. Optional: without it the page has no freshwater figure or layers |
| `chain.json` | `Chain` | food's emissions after the farm (FAOSTAT GPP, in `year`): `byCountry[iso3][stage][gas]`, kg per weighted tonne (`hfc`: the stage's fluorinated gases as kg of HFC-134a, from their AR5 CO₂e over 1,300), for processing, transport, packaging and retail (shared by `weights`, Poore & Nemecek's kg CO₂e per kg of each food at that stage) and for cooking, food waste and wastewater (shared by mass); `world`, the model's countries together, used for a country FAOSTAT has no figures for. Optional: without it nothing after the farm is counted |
| `origins.json` | `Origins` | where each country's imports of each food come from, from FAOSTAT's detailed trade matrix over `years`. `byCountry[iso3][fbsCode]` = `[origin iso3, share of the imports]` for the main origins, up to 95% of the imports, at most ten, and none under 1%. The rest of the imports take the world's average. Optional: without it, every import takes the world's average |
| `world.topo.json` | TopoJSON | Natural Earth admin-0, 1:50m, with `iso3`, `name` (English) and `nameFr` on each feature; the names label the places without a record in `countries.json` |

The thirteen lever files (`breakdown`, `practices`, `waste`, `deforestation`, `removals`, `deadfall`, `sectors`, `coemissions`, `industry`, `population`, `fossilmethane`, `wastemethane`, `hfc`, each declared by its module's `files`) are loaded with the rest. One that is missing (HTTP 404) is replaced by the empty value its module gives, so its levers have no effect and the interface shows them as not available. Without `population.json`, for example, the population option has no effect and the temperature chart draws no population. `enso.json`, `landuse.json`, `impacts.json`, `mammals.json`, `water.json`, `fair.json`, `solidwaste.json` and `oceanplastic.json` are optional core files (`OPTIONAL_FILES` in `app/src/data/load.ts`). Without the first, the temperature chart has no El Niño or La Niña marks; without the second, the map's land-use CO₂ layer has no data; without the third, Explore has no card on what the warming means; without the fourth, the summary has no farmed mammals figure and the map's farmed mammals layer has no data; without `water.json`, the summary has no freshwater figure and the two freshwater layers have no data; without `fair.json`, the temperature adds up each gas's AGTP instead of running FaIR; without `solidwaste.json` or `oceanplastic.json`, the waste layer's municipal or ocean plastic view has no data and the Waste chart has no municipal views or no plastic line. `climate.json`'s `attributionByGas` is optional too: a build without it hides the gas switch of the Causes of warming chart, which then shows every cause, and a build whose `attributionByGas` has no `hfc` leaves HFCs out of the switch. Any other file missing stops the app. A file its module marks `deferred` (`breakdown.json`, 2.2 MB, read by the farm practices only) is not loaded at start. Its empty value stands in for it until one of its module's levers is above 0, or until the panel works out what each lever is worth, in idle time after the page has settled. The interface does not wait for it to show those levers. As the app cannot know at start whether a deferred file is in the build, a practice lever stays available without `breakdown.json` and has no effect. `removals.json` rates are in tonnes of CO₂ per hectare a year, `deadfall.json` in kg of CO₂ per tonne of the item, `deforestation.json` in tonnes of CO₂ a year, its `areaByCountry` in hectares a year and its `drainedByCountry` (drained organic soils) in tonnes of CO₂ a year, `landuse.json` in tonnes of CO₂ a year (negative for a net sink, its `world` holding what the source assigns to no country), `population.json` in people (its ratios have no unit), `coemissions.json` in kg per kg of CO₂, W/m² per kg a year and kg of methane per kg of NOx, VOCs or CO. Without `coemissions.json` the energy and industry levers cut CO₂ alone. `fossilmethane.json` is in kg a year, kg in `cedsYear` and kg per kg of CO₂. Without `fossilmethane.json`, the methane leak levers are not available, the energy and industry levers count their methane in full, and the map's methane leaks layer has no data. `hfc.json` is in kg a year; without it, the HFC phase-down is not available.

`intensities.json` stores each distinct source once, sorted, in `sources`, and each cell's `source` is its index there (`IntensitiesFile`). The loader (`decodeIntensities` in `app/src/data/load.ts`) puts the URL back in each cell, so the model reads `Intensities`, whose `source` is the URL. Its `oilcake[iso3][oilseedCode][scope]` gives the kg of each gas a year that the cake of each crushed oilseed carries in the base year. This is the part of the crush's emissions that an oil taken from its seed leaves out (see `docs/methodology/en/emissions.md`). The loader keeps it in `Intensities`, and the model adds it to the country's farm emissions and changes it with the demand for the cake, as `oilcakeHa`. A build without it counts no cake emissions. Its `nonFood[iso3]` gives the kg of each gas a year from the country's animals raised but not farmed for food (draught cattle and buffalo, horses, asses and mules, cattle kept other than for food), the same in both scopes. The model adds it to the country's farm emissions, and no lever or shift changes it. A build without it has no such baseline.

Units: masses in tonnes for products and kilograms for gases, temperatures in °C relative to 1850–1900, years as integers. Country keys are ISO 3166-1 alpha-3, item keys are FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheet item codes as strings.

The fixtures in `app/public/fixtures/` (made by `npm run fixtures` in `app/`) follow the same types with a handful of countries and items, so the model and the interface are tested without the real data. `?data=fixtures` loads them in the app, and the end-to-end tests use it.
