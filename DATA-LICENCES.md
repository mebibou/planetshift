The road transport series in `sectors.json` are calibrated to CEDS's 2022 road transport CO₂ (v_2024_07_08, CC BY 4.0), the release the co-emissions are also built from. No EDGAR data is used.

Some parameters are single published values cited from articles that are not openly licensed (for example fuel use per tonne of fish from Parker et al. 2018, natural regrowth rates from Cook-Patton et al. 2020, and the reductions of several farming practices). They are reused as cited figures, with the reference on the Methodology page. The articles themselves are not redistributed.

Some sources add conditions to their licence. Keep these conditions when you reuse the files:

- **FAO** (FAOSTAT, FishStat): CC BY 4.0, and FAO's terms of use forbid using the data for, or with, the promotion of a commercial enterprise or its products. Cite as FAO asks: "FAO. [year]. [database]. Accessed on [date]. [URL]. Licence: CC BY 4.0."
- **Berkeley Earth**: CC BY-NC 4.0. Credit Berkeley Earth with a link to berkeleyearth.org. Commercial use needs Berkeley Earth's permission.
- **IEA**: its reports, charts and figures are under CC BY 4.0. Its standalone datasets and data annexes are not, and none of them is used here. The figures in `sectors.json` and `industry.json` are read from the reports and their charts. Credit as the IEA asks: "IEA [year], [title], [URL], Licence: CC BY 4.0".

When you reuse the data, credit Planetshift and the original sources listed in `meta.json`.

## Fonts

The interface's font, Lato, is self-hosted from `app/src/fonts/`. The files are WOFF2 copies of the Latin and Latin Extended subsets of its regular, italic, bold and black weights, from the Fontsource package `@fontsource/lato` 5.3.0. Lato is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose text is in `app/src/fonts/OFL.txt`. Each file keeps the font's copyright notice and Reserved Font Name in its metadata. The files are unmodified, so the name Lato is kept.
