Every source, its version and its licence are listed in `app/public/data/meta.json` and on the app's Methodology page.

The road transport series in `sectors.json` are calibrated to EDGAR's 2023 road transport CO₂ (6.1 billion tonnes, from the JRC's *GHG emissions of all world countries*, 2024 report). The IEA-EDGAR CO₂ data are licensed CC BY-NC-ND 4.0: non-commercial and no derivatives. Scaling a scenario to one published total may count as a derivative of the data, which that licence does not allow; this is unresolved, and the value should be cleared with the JRC or replaced by another source before `sectors.json` is redistributed.

Some parameters are single published values cited from articles that are not openly licensed (for example fuel use per tonne of fish from Parker et al. 2018, natural regrowth rates from Cook-Patton et al. 2020, and the reductions of several farming practices). They are reused as cited figures, with the reference on the Methodology page; the articles themselves are not redistributed. The UNEP Food Waste Index estimates are reused for non-profit purposes with acknowledgement, as UNEP's terms allow.

When reusing the data, credit Planetshift and the original sources listed in `meta.json`.
